Debrief

What Just Happened

  • 1 Context gets you better answers. A vague question gets a vague answer. A detailed stage gets a real performance.
  • 2 A role gives the AI a personality. "Tiny tactical hamster" is a role. The AI becomes that character and speaks from inside it.
  • 3 Specific in. Specific out. You can do this with any word, any topic, any subject. You are not looking things up in a dictionary. You are having a conversation.
Before Next Class

Build a Stage. Defend It.

Your Assignment

Write ONE prompt that sets the stage as clearly as possible. Give it a role. Give it context. Give it history. Make it strong.

Next class, Coach Shay will take your prompt and add ONE sentence designed to break it or confuse the AI. A weak stage collapses. A strong stage fights back.

WEAK: "You are a chef. What does the word hungry mean?"
STRONG: "You are a chef who grew up in a tiny village in France. You cooked for your grandmother every Sunday for fifteen years. Food is not just your job, it is your whole life. What does the word hungry mean to you?"

Write your prompt. Bring it next class. Let's see who built the strongest stage.
